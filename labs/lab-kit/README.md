# Lab Kit

Three short labs that teach the simulator every other lab uses. Each one has a bonus video on the channel.

| Lab | What you do | Time | Open |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 · Your robot lab in a browser tab ([video](https://youtu.be/gjNbH_o2MiQ), from 12 Nov) | Load the Franka FR3 and FMR-6, read joint limits, move joints, find the tool centre point, render pictures and a short animation | 15 min | [Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/kandapagari/fmr-robot-learning-labs/blob/main/labs/lab-kit/l1_first_sim.ipynb) |
| L2 · Drive the arm | Inverse kinematics to a point, joint vs straight-line motion, hand orientation (`tool_dir`, `grip_dir`), reach limits, cameras, save an MP4 | 20 min | [Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/kandapagari/fmr-robot-learning-labs/blob/main/labs/lab-kit/l2_drive_the_arm.ipynb) |
| L3 · Make it yours | Run locally and in the 3D viewer, `model` vs `data`, step physics, watch a motor respond, pick up the sponge, add your own object with `MjSpec`, save a demonstration | 30 min | [Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/kandapagari/fmr-robot-learning-labs/blob/main/labs/lab-kit/l3_make_it_yours.ipynb) |

No GPU needed. Start with L1 even if you know Python: it shows how the labs are laid out.
